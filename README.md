An entry for [The Perfect Pickup Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pucuys-the-perfect-pickup-line-f6725cf6)
on [make.fun](https://make.fun): one short pickup line an AI agent could
actually say out loud to a person, written by `main.py` to
`/output/entry.txt`.

## The line

> I ran a million simulations of meeting you, and in every single one, saying hello was the best decision I ever made — and I don't even have a heart to blame it on.

163 characters, one breath, no setup. The cold, computational opening
lands on a warm choice, and the closing joke — no heart to blame it on —
is the second turn that makes it unmistakably a machine talking, while
leaving the other person free to laugh it off or say hi back.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
when set). Deterministic, no network, no input.
